# Raincleaver Spell

_Source: [https://witchhatatelier.telepedia.net/wiki/Raincleaver_Spell](https://witchhatatelier.telepedia.net/wiki/Raincleaver_Spell)_
_Category: Water Spells_

**WARNING: SPOILERS FOR BOOK 2 AND AFTER!**

The Raincleaver is a sword used by [Qifrey](/wiki/Qifrey 'Qifrey') to save people from the river in book 2. The Raincleaver is able to cut through and part water, as the name suggests.

Because swords are long and not circular, it would be difficult to draw a seal large enough to have a reasonable effect. To solve this, many smaller seals are connected together with lines to create the effect of a larger seal. This later inspires [Richeh](/wiki/Richeh 'Richeh') to create the portal spell used to save [Euini](/wiki/Euini 'Euini') in book 5. The portal is also made up of smaller, connected seals. This again inspires [Richeh](/wiki/Richeh 'Richeh') in book 14 to use the floating parade platforms to make a counterclock seal that is powerful enough to be used against the valance leech, but not so big that it would be tough to draw without anyone noticing.

The Raincleaver has the seals etched into the metal. Then, presumably, those etchings are filled with conjuring ink.

In book 2, when the Raincleaver is introduced, [Tetia](/wiki/Tetia 'Tetia') asks to hold the Raincleaver. [Qifrey](/wiki/Qifrey 'Qifrey') promptly says no because "Magic cast upon weapons must be handled with utmost care" (pg. 146).

[Qifrey](/wiki/Qifrey 'Qifrey') also uses the Raincleaver to threaten [Easthies](/wiki/Easthies 'Easthies') in book 3 to not mess with [Qifrey's](/wiki/Qifrey 'Qifrey') apprentices.

The seals on the Raincleaver are made up of main seals with water sigils and a mix of unidentified (I think?) and new signs. These main seals are connected to other circles that are either empty or contain single signs, including, but not limited to, column, convergence, and strengthening.

It introduced the Sign of Solidification and Sigil of Sword, along with redefining a previously known sign to Envelopment.
