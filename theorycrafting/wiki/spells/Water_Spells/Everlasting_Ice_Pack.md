**Everlasting Ice Pack** is a water-type spell that creates an "ice pack" made from running water.

## Description

Contrary to its name, the everlasting ice pack is not an ice spell, instead it relies on a water sigil and a single column sign pointing towards it. In the same panel, it is partially seen that Coco's attempt at creating it used many signs of columns, pointing at each other in a circular formation.

## Usage

Coco's intention was to give the ice pack to Qifrey, as he was still in the Healing Spire at the Great Hall. While working on it in the cafeteria, Olruggio saw it and gave input on how to make it less explosive. Eventually, it was delivered to Qifrey, who awoke to find it in a bowl near him. Olruggio explained that it was Coco's invention, a work in progress due to its tendency to make Qifrey toss and turn.
