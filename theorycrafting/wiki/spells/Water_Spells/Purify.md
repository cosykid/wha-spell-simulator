**Purify**[*unofficial name*] is a water-type [spell](/wiki/Spells 'Spells') that purifies water.

## Description

Purify is a spell that consists of a central water sigil enclosed by two [collection signs](/wiki/Signs_Explained#Collection 'Signs Explained') on either side, and surrounded by [purify signs](/wiki/Signs_Explained#Purify 'Signs Explained') above and below. The seal purifies wastewater.

## Usage

[Coco](/wiki/Coco 'Coco') debuts this spell at the [Silver Eve](/wiki/Silver_Eve 'Silver Eve') festival during the [Silver Eve Procession](/wiki/Silver_Eve_Procession 'Silver Eve Procession') drawn on the [Portable Waste Purification Pot](/wiki/Portable_Waste_Purification_Pot 'Portable Waste Purification Pot'). [Coco](/wiki/Coco 'Coco') pours wastewater into the large pot and showcases how the pot purifies and cleans the water thanks to the seal to the crowd.
