**Light Tracer** is the spell of the [Crystal Fragment Bangles](/wiki/Crystal_Fragment_Bangles 'Crystal Fragment Bangles') created by [Richeh](/wiki/Richeh 'Richeh') as her [Contraption](/wiki/Contraption 'Contraption') for [Silver Eve](/wiki/Silver_Eve 'Silver Eve').

## Description

Having the same basis of the [Boulder Stretch Rope](/wiki/Boulder_Stretch_Rope 'Boulder Stretch Rope'), but with a light sigil replacing the earth one and with a extra sign at the top with an unknown effect, but it is assumed to make the connection between the rings. If an item is broken apart and a light tracer seal is drawn on one of it's fragments, the spell will send a string of light to all other fragments of that object, this effect being visible in the crystal fragment bangles.

## Usage

Until chapter 55, the spell or the rings it is attached too have not been used by anyone, not even [Richeh](/wiki/Richeh 'Richeh')
