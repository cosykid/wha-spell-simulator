**Forbidden glow** is a [spell](/wiki/Spells 'Spells') of unknown type which creates glowing sparkles of light.

## Description

This spell is composed of a ring of column signs with an unknown central sigil. It's effect appears to be similar to that of a light-based spell, but whether or not the spell is forbidden is unknown.

## Usage

This spell was the first one [Coco](/wiki/Coco 'Coco') ever drew, copied from the [picture book](/wiki/Magic_Book 'Magic Book') sold to her by [Iguin](/wiki/Iguin 'Iguin'). Other spells Coco is known to have copied include [forbidden flames](/wiki/Forbidden_Flames 'Forbidden Flames') and [petrification](/wiki/Petrification 'Petrification').
