**Scalewolf Curse** is a forbidden spell that permanently causes someone's body to be transformed. In the case of [Euini](/wiki/Euini 'Euini'), he was turned into a [Scalewolf](/wiki/Scalewolf 'Scalewolf').

## Description

The spell contains a sigil identical to one in the [Illusory Labyrinth](/wiki/Illusory_Labyrinth 'Illusory Labyrinth') spell, this sigil being made up of three dots arranged in a triangle shape, but other than that, all of its sigils and signs are unknown. When it was used on Euini, the spell transformed him into a scalewolf. This happened as a result of the [Mirror Cloak of Borrowshade](/wiki/Mirror_Cloak_of_Borrowshade 'Mirror Cloak of Borrowshade') he was wearing fusing with him, as the cloak contained the fur and scales of a scalewolf.

## Usage

The spell's first and only known use was by [Sasaran](/wiki/Sasaran 'Sasaran'), a Brimmed Cap Witch, during the events at [Serpentback Cave](/wiki/Serpentback_Cave_Arc 'Serpentback Cave Arc'). Sasaran used the spell on Euini, forcibly fusing him with his cloak, resulting in his transforming into a scalewolf.
