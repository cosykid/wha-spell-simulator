**Illusory labyrinth** is a forbidden [spell](/wiki/Spells 'Spells') that creates a labyrinthine area that loops infinitely, making it impossible to escape.

## Description

The spell contains the same central sigil as the [Scalewolf Curse](/wiki/Scalewolf_Curse 'Scalewolf Curse'), with column and directional signs, the remaining signs and sigils all being unidentified. The spell creates a grand underground labyrinth within which even space is distorted, as if you walk too far you will show up right where you started.

## Usage

The spell is responsible for the formation of the [underground labyrinth](/wiki/Draconic_Labyrinth_Arc 'Draconic Labyrinth Arc'), which [Iguin](/wiki/Iguin 'Iguin') trapped all of [Qifrey's Apprentices](/wiki/Qifrey%27s_Atelier "Qifrey's Atelier") inside of. The spell was guarded by a [dragon](/wiki/Dragon 'Dragon'), which the apprentices had to get past using the [Serpent's Bed of Sand](/wiki/Serpent%27s_Bed_of_Sand "Serpent's Bed of Sand") spell they created, after which they destroyed the spell and were extracted by [Qifrey](/wiki/Qifrey 'Qifrey') from the labyrinth just in time before it collapsed.
