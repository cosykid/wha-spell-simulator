**Floating expansion** is a niche [spell](/wiki/Spells 'Spells') that makes the object it's drawn on levitate and grow.

## Description

The spell contains a central window sigil, surrounded by enlarging signs. Float and column signs are located in the gaps between the enlarging signs. The spell causes whatever it's drawn on to grow to many times it's original size, as well as causing said object to levitate.

## Usage

The spell was used by [Qifrey](/wiki/Qifrey 'Qifrey'), who had it drawn inside a tiny magic book he owned. He used the spell to enlarge the book, and with Coco's help, they used the book as a vehicle to evade a [guardian of the tower](/wiki/The_Guardian_of_the_Tower_of_Tomes 'The Guardian of the Tower of Tomes') at the [Tower of Tomes](/wiki/Tower_of_Tomes 'Tower of Tomes').

## Trivia

An object with this spell on it was included in the [Volume 7](/wiki/Volume_7 'Volume 7') limited edition.
