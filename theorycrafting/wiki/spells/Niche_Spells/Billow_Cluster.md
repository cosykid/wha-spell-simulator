**Billow Cluster** is a niche [spell](/wiki/Spells 'Spells') that creates a cloud out of any material that is directed into it.

## Description

Billow cluster contains a central billowing sigil surrounded by collection signs. It collects material directed into it and converts said material into a cloud.

## Usage

The billow cluster seal was used in the [serpent's bed of sand](/wiki/Serpent%27s_Bed_of_Sand "Serpent's Bed of Sand") seal, where it served to convert the sand generated by the other parts of the spell into a cloud upon which the [dragon](/wiki/Dragon 'Dragon') hunting the [girls](/wiki/Qifrey%27s_Atelier "Qifrey's Atelier") slept, allowing them the time to destroy the [illusory labyrinth](/wiki/Illusory_Labyrinth 'Illusory Labyrinth') seal which was preventing their escape.
