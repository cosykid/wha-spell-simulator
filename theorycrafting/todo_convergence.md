# Convergence — RESOLVED (2026-07-03)

The proposal that used to live here was ruled on and promoted to ground truth:
**GROUND_TRUTH.md §8** (lens scaling in the composed field §9, scorecard rows
§10, leftover unknowns §11.10–12). Convergence is the first **modifier** sign:
column = engine, region = valve, levitation = spring, pull = ambient coupling,
**convergence = lens** — a scalar focus budget $Q = \sum\lambda_i$ that tightens
every envelope the seal emits, boosts speeds through the narrower throat, and
rigidifies the packed core. Implemented as the `convergence` sign kind + the
excluded-volume / rigidity transport mechanics.

## Rulings

1. **Lens wins.** Not a fourth coupling (no focal spring — that would be a
   poor-man's levitation and invent unattested self-holding balls), not a
   spawn pin. Focus acts on the _fields_; the bare-focus seal is a candle
   plume, not a held ball and not a point source.
2. **Regular triangle, λ = glyph scale.** The triangle always stays
   equilateral, so λ is the overall size (side length in seal units), not a
   height measured along some axis. Position = centroid. Size alters strength
   (wiki), so λ enters Q linearly — unlike region, whose size stays unread.
3. **Rigidity, with excluded volume.** Velocity relaxation toward the local
   mean at rate ∝ Q (the SBOS "packs tightly, somewhat rigid" line — the one
   direct canon sentence). Corollary ruling: particles need a **repulsive
   short-range field** so magic behaves like it has volume — otherwise a
   focused core would collapse to a point. Global mechanic, every seal; also
   fixes the waterball's slow squish (the ball now keeps a content-set size).
4. **One-sided, orientation ignored.** No canon example of inversion, and the
   equilateral glyph is 120°-symmetric — "apex direction" is ill-defined, so
   the law reads ink amount only. No anti-focus mode. This supersedes the
   cos-weighted radial aggregation from ruling 2's parse: there is no
   direction to weight. Wall-of-wind (the wiki's semi-directional evidence)
   stays an open question (§11.10).
5. **Seal-wide scope.** The lens focuses the ambient pull field too — u_amb
   is the spell's own magic acting at a distance (§7 precedent). A focused
   pull ring inhales through a narrower, more axial throat.
6. **Lev composition accepted.** Ball shrinks by F, becomes rigid and denser;
   W_max untouched (capacity belongs to C_lev). The pyreball → floatglow-lamp
   contrast is exactly this delta.

## Deviations from the proposal as discussed

- R4 overrides the proposal's (and R2's) directional parse: the recommended
  cos-weighted radial aggregation with signed inversion was dropped for a
  pure scalar Q — simpler, and honest about the glyph's symmetry. In the
  model, convergence is effectively _non-directional_ despite the wiki class.
- R3 gained the **excluded-volume** mechanic (not in the proposal): a global
  density pressure for all tracers, proposed by the user during the ruling.
- Evidence weighting as proposed: SBOS dialogue A-grade; lamp + sylph shoes
  B-grade (the lev pairing is the load-bearing pattern); water dragon's
  "gathers clouds" reading discounted (composite seal, collection's turf);
  raincleaver/twinned bottles/beacon discounted (linked/ancient).
