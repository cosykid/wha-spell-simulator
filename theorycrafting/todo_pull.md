# Pull — RESOLVED (2026-07-03)

The proposal that used to live here was ruled on and promoted to ground truth:
the law, parsing, evidence anchors and predictions are now **GROUND_TRUTH.md §7**
(ambient population in the composed field §8, scorecard rows §9, leftover
unknowns §10.8–9). Pull is the **third coupling**: column → own element flow,
levitation → bodies, pull → ambient matter of the seal's element. Implemented
in the sim as the `pull` sign kind + the `AmbientMedium` population (grasp
latch, capacitor throttle); presets 24–29 (grasping wind ×2, pinwheel, repel,
vacuum snout, earth conveyor) carry the executable checks.

## Rulings

1. **Tail parsing, full length.** Application point = the bare tail end,
   direction = tail → tip, λ = whole tail-to-tip length. Simplest, uniform
   with column/levitation (apply at the rear, point along the arrow). The
   water-cage spanning-sign puzzle that motivated fancier parses is a
   _linked two-seal spell_ — out of scope, and linking may have its own rules.
2. **Ambient only.** Pull never tugs the seal's own manifestation (else
   grasping wind swallows its own burst, flame burst self-defeats).
   Population tags never change.
3. **Region gates the field, not the matter.** $u_{\text{amb}}$ passes the §5
   exhaust pipeline (it _is_ the spell's magic acting at distance); the mask
   never applies to foreign matter. Unlocks the directional-intake
   (vacuum snout) composition.
4. **Push = signed burst kernel.** Inverted pull is the sink's time-reverse
   (hemispherical, up on axis), not a column-style planar fan — pull has no
   clash bookkeeping.
5. **Capacitor grasp.** Pulled matter pools on the disk (stop-aging) and
   counts against $G_{max} \propto \eta R^2 C_{p,+}$; the pull field throttles
   by the remaining fraction — charges fast, then dies away. Self-limiting.
   (Wall bend was judged _not_ decisive for plain pooling — extra unknown
   signs on that seal.) Push/conveyor modes gather nothing, never self-limit.
6. **Thin-everywhere medium.** Every element is seeded thinly through the
   domain so the ambient medium visibly exists; no reservoir tool in v1.
7. **The seal gets yanked — into the ground.** Reaction pair is real at law
   level; the sim's substrate is anchored to immovable ground, so it stays
   unmodeled. Movable substrates remain the chapter shared with skysoaring.
8. **Pull-only seals manifest nothing (2026-07-03, post-promotion).** If pull
   is the seal's only budget-bearing ink (no column, no levitation), nothing
   manifests — no shockwave, no own population; the ambient coupling is the
   whole spell (pure manipulate mode, the first sign-grammar instance of §1's
   create-vs-manipulate). Column/levitation restore manifestation — pinned by
   the new "Flame burst — column + pull ring" preset (canon coexistence);
   region/convergence do NOT (valve and lens act on the ambient field and
   manifest nothing themselves), so vacuum snout and focused intake stay
   silent. Supersedes the "own burst untouched, populations counter-stream"
   reading grasping wind carried under R2 — R2's exemption still governs
   wherever manifestation exists. Implemented as `Nozzle.manifests`, gating
   the burst term and own-tracer spawning. New open question (GT §11.13):
   does a low-quality fizzle burst anyway?

## Deviations from the proposal as discussed

- R1: the proposal recommended parsing at **stem ∩ triangle base** (crossbar
  analog, water-cage-friendly); the ruling chose tail + full length for
  simplicity and demoted water cage to linked-seal evidence.
- R5 gained the **capacity/capacitor** mechanic (not in the proposal), a
  deliberate mirror of levitation's fill-to-capacity ruling.
- R6 chose thin-everywhere over the proposal's placeable reservoir blob.
- R7 dropped even the readout arrow: anchored substrate absorbs the yank.
